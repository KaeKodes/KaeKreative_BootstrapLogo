Kae Kreative Logo System

Four variations, one wordmark, built to work on light and dark backgrounds.

Logo variations

1. Black and white

Solid black, no offset. Use for one-color print, stamps, fax, and engraving. On dark surfaces the mark is inverted to white.

2. Color (primary)

Default brand mark for websites, social, and slides. The dark wordmark needs a light background; on dark, use the reverse version.

3. Reverse

White KREATIVE with pink KAE for dark photos, dark UI, and video. Do not place on light backgrounds; the faded example shows why.

How to use the logo

Clear space

Leave space around the logo equal to the height of the KAE letters on every side. Keep text, images, and edges outside that zone.

Minimum size

  • Screen: 120 px wide minimum for the full wordmark.
  • Print: 1 inch (25 mm) wide minimum.
  • Below those sizes, use the favicon mark.

Do and don’t

  • Do use the SVG files so edges stay sharp at any size.
  • Do choose the variation that matches the background contrast.
  • Don’t stretch, rotate, or recolor the wordmark.
  • Don’t place the color version on pink, or the reverse version on white.
